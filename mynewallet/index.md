Straight Answers

## The Questions Worth Asking.

### Is MyneWallet free?

Yes. MyneWallet is free to download and the core budgeting and tracking engine is free permanently — no advertisements, no trial expiry. PRO is an optional one-time purchase that removes every limit. It is not a subscription and it never renews.

### Does MyneWallet work offline?

Completely. MyneWallet is offline-first by architecture. There is no account and no sign-in, and every feature — including the full analysis engine — works with the device in airplane mode. The app touches the network only if you personally enable Google Drive backup or make a purchase through Google Play.

### Does MyneWallet connect to my bank?

No. MyneWallet never asks for bank credentials and uses no aggregation service. That is deliberate twice over: continuous read access to your accounts is the single largest privacy concession in personal finance software, and the two seconds spent logging a purchase are the moment you actually notice it. The app is engineered to make those two seconds as fast as physically possible, not to remove them.

### Where is my financial data stored?

In an encrypted SQLite database inside your device’s private application sandbox, which Android isolates from every other app. The Brink Labs operates no servers holding your financial data, so no copy of your ledger exists outside your phone unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

### What happens if I lose my phone?

You restore from a backup you control. MyneWallet encrypts your entire vault on-device with AES-256 and a randomised initialisation vector, then uploads that ciphertext to your own Google Drive. Restoring on a new phone is a two-tap operation. You can also export a plain CSV at any time and keep it wherever you like.

### Is MyneWallet a subscription?

No. PRO is a single one-time unlock tied to your Google account. It never expires, it survives a reinstall and a new phone, and new PRO capabilities added in future updates are included rather than sold again.

### What is zero-based budgeting?

Zero-based budgeting means assigning every unit of income a specific job before it is spent, until the amount left to assign reaches zero. Instead of reviewing what you spent after the fact, you decide in advance what each dollar is for. MyneWallet implements it with envelopes, an Auto Assign tool, and a live To Assign counter that reads zero when the plan is complete.

### Can I import my history from another budgeting app?

Yes. MyneWallet imports CSV files and adopts the categories it finds automatically, ranked by how material each one actually is, so your history arrives intact rather than collapsing into an uncategorised bucket. Export is equally complete — CSV for your raw data, PDF for a formatted statement.

### Does MyneWallet show advertisements or track me?

No. There is no advertising network, no attribution SDK and no product-analytics SDK in the app, and Google’s Firebase suite is not integrated. Do not take our word for it: put the app behind a network monitor, or simply use it in airplane mode, where nothing degrades.

### Which platforms does MyneWallet support?

Android, through Google Play. There is no web version and no cross-device sync — a direct and deliberate consequence of storing your ledger only on your own device.

### How does MyneWallet make money if it does not sell my data?

From one-time PRO unlocks, and from nothing else. There is no advertising inventory, no data brokerage, no affiliate placement and no recurring revenue to protect, which is precisely why the app can afford to work entirely offline. A business model that depends on your attention or your ledger produces different software, and the difference is visible in every screen.

### Do I need an account to use MyneWallet?

No. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. You open the app and start budgeting. An account exists to identify you to a server, and there is no server here to be identified to — the only optional Google sign-in is for backing up an already-encrypted file to your own Google Drive.

### What are the limits of the free version of MyneWallet?

The free tier allows 3 active accounts, 7 active envelopes, 10 active budget categories and 4 active obligations, with a 3-month rolling ledger and 1 month of analytics in each of the Category, Nature, Tag and Heatmap sections. Tags are unlimited, nothing is time-limited, and there are no advertisements. The one-time PRO unlock removes every one of those caps and extends analytics to 12 months per section alongside full historical ledger analysis.

### Is there an iPhone version of MyneWallet?

Not currently. MyneWallet is an Android application distributed through Google Play, and there is no iOS release, no web application and no desktop client at this time.

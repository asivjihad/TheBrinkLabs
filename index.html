The Industry Standard

Three Business Models. All Of Them Aimed At You.

MODEL 01

Sell The Attention

Advertising turns every screen into inventory. The product is improved for the advertiser who wants a longer session.

MODEL 02

Sell The Behaviour

Aggregate what people do, categorise it, and license the pattern. Your data sits on somebody else's server, one breach from everyone.

MODEL 03

Rent The Access

Charge monthly for a tool you already learned. Stop paying and your own history locks behind a paywall.

There is a fourth model. You sell the software.

It is the oldest one, it is the only one with no hidden second customer. We took it back.

The Doctrine

Four Laws. Every Product. No Exceptions.

LAW I

On-Device By Default

If a feature can run on the phone, it runs on the phone. The network is a privilege we ask permission for.

LAW II

Owned, Not Rented

You pay once. The software is then yours through reinstalls, new phones, and every update we ship.

LAW III

Zero Telemetry

No advertising SDK. No product analytics. We find out whether something works by asking people.

LAW IV

Correctness Before Cleverness

Money is stored as integers. Every write is atomic. We test the arithmetic harder than the animation.
